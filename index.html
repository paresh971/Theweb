📚 Green Technology Knowledge Hub

Core benchmarks, carbon emission metrics, and daily actionable reduction knowledge.

🌎 Daily Carbon Targets

The global average footprint is ~12 kg CO₂e/day. To limit global warming under climate targets, individual daily limits must drop below 5.5 kg CO₂e/day.

🚗 Transport Impact

Petrol vehicles produce around 0.21 kg CO₂e per km. Swapping 10 km of car commuting for public transit saves roughly 1.3 kg of CO₂ every single day.

⚡ Energy & Grid Intensity

Standard coal-heavy electricity grids emit ~0.85 kg CO₂e per kWh. Solar and wind energy lower lifecycle emissions down to ~0.04 kg CO₂e per kWh.

🍔 Plant-Based Transition

Diet choices carry a significant carbon footprint. Moving from a heavy meat diet (7.2 kg/day) to a vegan diet (2.9 kg/day) saves over 1.5 metric tons of CO₂ annually.

🌳 Tree Offset Mechanics

A mature tree absorbs roughly 21.77 kg CO₂ per year (~0.06 kg/day). Planting trees directly compensates long-term residual carbon footprints.

🗑️ Waste Management

Sending organic waste to landfills releases methane gas (~0.80 kg CO₂e/day). Composting food waste cuts landfill methane emissions down to near zero (~0.05 kg CO₂e/day).
